- [Covers][esmporium.requirements.Covers] replaced `pad_years` and `ideal_pad_years`
  with `pad` and `ideal_pad`, pairs of [Span][esmporium.datasets.Span], and requires
  `align`, `mode` and `pad` to be stated. Every stored requirement's hash changed with
  it, so a [StoredRequirement][esmporium.db.StoredRequirement] written before this will
  not be found again -- clear or re-stem any tracked search before its next run.
- [CatalogueWithTimeInfo][esmporium.requirements.CatalogueWithTimeInfo] answers with
  [VersionTimeRange][esmporium.datasets.VersionTimeRange] and
  [BranchPoint][esmporium.datasets.BranchPoint], or `None` where nothing has recorded
  one, instead of fractional years.
- A constraint which raises now ends the solve, unless it raises
  [ConstraintNotEvaluableError][esmporium.requirements.ConstraintNotEvaluableError].
  Any exception used to become an unsatisfied group.
