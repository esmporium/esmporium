A lineage `find_parents` has not walked yet lands in
[SolveResult.couldnt_answer][esmporium.requirements.SolveResult] as `NEEDS_FIND_PARENTS`.

- [SolveResult.find_parents_inputs][esmporium.requirements.SolveResult.find_parents_inputs]
  hands back the version ids to pass to `find_parents`.
- [InMemoryCatalogue][esmporium.requirements.InMemoryCatalogue] applies the
  `VersionFilter` to each entry, through
  [passes_version_filter][esmporium.requirements.passes_version_filter].
