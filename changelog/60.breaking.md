Removed `esmporium.query.as_query_iterable`. The functions it normalised queries for ([search][esmporium.search.search] and [check_query_values][esmporium.search.check_query_values]) now take a [Requirement][esmporium.requirements.Requirement] rather than one-or-more queries, so nothing called it. [AllSubQueriesFailedError][esmporium.search.AllSubQueriesFailedError] also now requires `labels`, so every failure it reports says which leaf and project it belongs to.
