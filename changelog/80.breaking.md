Time points and lengths of time have new names and carry more of their own rules.

- `Position` was renamed [TimePoint][esmporium.datasets.TimePoint].
- `Span` was replaced by [TimeDelta][esmporium.datasets.TimeDelta], which is signed
  and has an optional `calendar`. `Covers` writes the new `calendar` field into its
  JSON, so every stored requirement's hash changes.
  `NegativeSpanError` became `NegativeLengthError`, which `Covers` raises (wrapped by
  pydantic) for negative padding or overlap. `FractionalCalendarFreeSpanError` became
  `FractionalCalendarFreeDeltaError`.
- [TimeRange][esmporium.datasets.TimeRange] holds two `TimePoint`s. Build one from
  numbers with [TimeRange.from_values][esmporium.datasets.TimeRange.from_values].
- `esmporium.datasets.calendars` was folded into `esmporium.datasets.time`.
  `advance` and `retreat` became
  [TimePoint.plus][esmporium.datasets.TimePoint.plus], `convert_position`
  became [convert_time_point][esmporium.datasets.convert_time_point], `position_of_date`
  became
  [TimePoint.from_year_and_month][esmporium.datasets.TimePoint.from_year_and_month],
  and `check_position` became `check_time_point_value`.
- `SPAN_BY_REPORTING_INTERVAL` and `span_of_reporting_interval` were renamed
  [DELTA_BY_REPORTING_INTERVAL][esmporium.enrich.DELTA_BY_REPORTING_INTERVAL] and
  [delta_of_reporting_interval][esmporium.enrich.delta_of_reporting_interval].
