Saving results into a SQLite database now requires the engine to be configured with [configure_sqlite_for_concurrency][esmporium.db.configure_sqlite_for_concurrency], whether the search runs serially or in parallel, to avoid the possibility for partial writes. [ingest_parsed_documents][esmporium.db.ingest_parsed_documents], [save_dataset][esmporium.db.save_dataset] and the processors built from them raise [UnconfiguredSQLiteEngineError][esmporium.db.UnconfiguredSQLiteEngineError] otherwise. Call it straight after creating the engine, e.g. `configure_sqlite_for_concurrency(create_engine(url))`
