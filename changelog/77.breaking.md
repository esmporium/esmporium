The time values now hold only what was published, and every guess is an input.

- [BranchPoint][esmporium.datasets.BranchPoint] keeps a published branch time as
  published, float and all, and gained `child_axis` for `in_child`. A position on a
  yearly or monthly axis has to be a whole number.
- [read_time_axis][esmporium.enrich.read_time_axis] raises
  [NoCalendarError][esmporium.enrich.NoCalendarError] when the time variable names no
  calendar, rather than assuming `standard`. `CF_DEFAULT_CALENDAR` was removed.
- [PublishedRange][esmporium.enrich.PublishedRange] holds the two stamps and any
  suffix. `to_half_open` takes `precision` and `reports_every`, places a range on a
  yearly or monthly axis only, and raises for daily or finer stamps.
  `is_climatology` became `suffix`, and filename readers no longer take
  `reporting_interval`.
- [TimeRange.extent][esmporium.datasets.TimeRange.extent] returns a
  `Span`. Two axes are comparable only when their units
  match and, for dated axes, both name the same calendar. Use
  `convert_position` to move between axes.
- Renamed `measure_version` to
  [combine_file_ranges][esmporium.enrich.combine_file_ranges], and
  `NoFilesToMeasureError` to `NoFilesToCombineError`.
