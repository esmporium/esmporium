- [Catalogue][esmporium.requirements.Catalogue] now has `parent_of`, so a catalogue of
  your own has to provide it before a requirement with a lineage can be solved against
  it.
- Every stored requirement's hash changed, because
  [Leaf][esmporium.requirements.Leaf] gained `lineage` and
  [requirement_hash][esmporium.requirements.Requirement.requirement_hash] covers the
  whole tree. A [StoredRequirement][esmporium.db.StoredRequirement] written before this
  will not be found again.
