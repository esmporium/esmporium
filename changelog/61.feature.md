Added [esmporium.db.summarise_search][] which takes the output of [esmporium.db.search_tracking][] (or [esmporium.db.rerun_search_esgf][]) and produces the summary of what a tracked search returned. The summary carries what changed and what the search returns now, either as its own types or, via [esmporium.db.SearchSummary.changes_frame][] and [esmporium.db.SearchSummary.current_frame][], as [pandas.DataFrame][]s.
