[esmporium.headers][] reads from a byte source rather than from a fixed prefix of
bytes.

- [read_global_attributes][esmporium.headers.read_global_attributes] takes a
  [ByteSource][esmporium.headers.ByteSource] instead of `prefix` and `file_size`.
  `read_netcdf3_global_attributes` and `read_hdf5_global_attributes` became
  [read_netcdf3_file_header][esmporium.headers.read_netcdf3_file_header] and
  [read_hdf5_file_header][esmporium.headers.read_hdf5_file_header], which return a
  [FileHeader][esmporium.headers.FileHeader].
- `FileHeader.attributes` was renamed `global_attributes`. `HeaderBytes` was removed.
- `want_bytes` and `max_want_bytes` on
  [read_header][esmporium.headers.read_header], `read_one_header`, `read_headers` and
  [build_header_facts_provider][esmporium.db.build_header_facts_provider] became
  `block_bytes` and `max_bytes`, with `max_requests` beside them on the first three.
  `DEFAULT_WANT_BYTES` and `DEFAULT_MAX_WANT_BYTES` became `DEFAULT_BLOCK_BYTES` and
  `DEFAULT_MAX_BYTES`.
- [TruncatedHeaderError][esmporium.headers.TruncatedHeaderError] takes `reason`
  instead of `bytes_given`.
- `HeaderParser`, `PARSERS_BY_LAYOUT` and `HeaderParserUnavailableError` were renamed
  [HeaderReader][esmporium.headers.HeaderReader],
  [READERS_BY_LAYOUT][esmporium.headers.READERS_BY_LAYOUT] and
  [HeaderReaderUnavailableError][esmporium.headers.HeaderReaderUnavailableError].
- netCDF-4 files are read with `h5netcdf`, which the `headers` extra now installs.
