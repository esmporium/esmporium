Removed the per-column indexes on `Dataset`.
The only thing a `Dataset` has to have to itself is its ID.
Two datasets that describe the same data under different IDs are allowed,
because the IDs come from ESGF rather than from us.
How we tell you about such a clash is still being worked out.

Every facet of a `Dataset` is now required.
A dataset that is missing its variable or its experiment
isn't a partial record, it's one we can't identify.

Setting a field on a `Dataset` to something of the wrong type
now raises straight away, rather than failing later when the dataset is saved.

`esmporium` also now keeps its tables in its own registry,
so they no longer appear in the databases
of applications that happen to use `SQLModel` themselves.
