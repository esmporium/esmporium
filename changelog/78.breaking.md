[Covers][esmporium.requirements.Covers] lines runs up through their branch points only.

- `Covers` has no `align` field, so every stored requirement's hash changes.
- [BranchPoint][esmporium.datasets.BranchPoint] holds each end as a
  [Position][esmporium.datasets.Position]: `in_parent`, and `in_child` or `None`.
- `CatalogueWithTimeInfo.get_branch_time_in_parent` is renamed `get_branch_point`.
