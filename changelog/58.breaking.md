- [Catalogue.find][esmporium.requirements.Catalogue.find] now takes a keyword-only needs_facets, so a catalogue of your own has to accept it. Ignoring it is correct: it says what will be read, not what must be skipped.
- A [CatalogueEntry][esmporium.requirements.CatalogueEntry] from a solve answers only for the facets the requirement named. Asking it for another raises [UnloadedFacetError][esmporium.requirements.UnloadedFacetError] rather than None, which would be indistinguishable from a dataset recording no value. Name the facet in the requirement to have it read.
