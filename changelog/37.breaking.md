- Renamed `NoAPIAnsweredError` to [NoFacadeAnsweredError][emsporium.search.NoFacadeAnsweredError] and its `failures` argument should now be a dictionary, keyed by [FacadeKey][emsporium.search.search.FacadeKey] rather than a tuple

- [check_query_values_low][emsporium.search.check_query_values_low] now expects to receive [FacadeKey][emsporium.search.search.FacadeKey] rather than `source` (a plain string)
- [ResultProcessor][emsporium.search.ResultProcessor] now receives a [SearchAPIFacade][emsporium.search.SearchAPIFacade] as its first argument, rather than a plain string
