- Renamed `NoAPIAnsweredError` to [NoFacadeAnsweredError][esmporium.search.NoFacadeAnsweredError] and its `failures` argument should now be a dictionary, keyed by [FacadeKey][esmporium.search.search.FacadeKey] rather than a tuple
- [ValueCheckOutcome][esmporium.search.ValueCheckOutcome] now keys its `reports` and `failures` by [FacadeKey][esmporium.search.search.FacadeKey], rather than host
- [SearchOutcome][esmporium.search.SearchOutcome]'s `datasets` attribute has been renamed to `parsed_docs`. [SearchOutcome][esmporium.search.SearchOutcome] now keys its `parsed_docs`, `n_matches` and `failures` by [FacadeKey][esmporium.search.search.FacadeKey], rather than host
- [CouldNotSearchError][esmporium.search.search.CouldNotSearchError] (and its subclasses) now take and carry a [FacadeKey][esmporium.search.search.FacadeKey] as `facade_key`, rather than a `host` string
- [ValueReport][esmporium.search.ValueReport]'s `source` is now a [FacadeKey][esmporium.search.search.FacadeKey], rather than a plain string
- [check_query_values_low][esmporium.search.check_query_values_low] now expects to receive [FacadeKey][esmporium.search.search.FacadeKey] rather than `source` (a plain string)
- [ResultProcessor][esmporium.search.ResultProcessor] now receives a [SearchAPIFacade][esmporium.search.SearchAPIFacade] as its first argument, rather than a plain string
