Time points can be subtracted and shifted, with the checks done by the types.

- [TimePoint.delta_from][esmporium.datasets.TimePoint.delta_from] gives the
  [TimeDelta][esmporium.datasets.TimeDelta] between two time points, tagged with
  their calendar. Two dated axes in the same calendar may count from different dates.
- [TimePoint.plus][esmporium.datasets.TimePoint.plus] moves a time point by a
  `TimeDelta`. Years or months measured in one calendar are refused on an axis in
  another; days, hours and minutes are walked in any calendar.
- [TimeDelta.in_steps][esmporium.datasets.TimeDelta.in_steps] converts between years
  and months, between days, hours and minutes, and between the two where the calendar
  fixes the length, e.g. 730 `noleap` days is two years.
- [Covers][esmporium.requirements.Covers] accepts padding and overlap stated in a
  calendar, e.g. `TimeDelta(20, TimeStep.YEARS, "noleap")`. Against data in another
  calendar the requirement fails, or is degraded for an ideal, with a message saying
  how to restate the length. Monthly and yearly data count months whatever the
  calendar, so the calendar does not matter there.
